About me

Engineer by training. Builder by nature.

Who I am

Hi, I'm Shadow.

I'm a Senior Computer Engineering student at the American University of Bahrain (AUBH). My journey into engineering started with a simple question, how does this actually work?, and grew into a passion for building technology from the ground up.

Through my degree I've moved from logic gates and circuits to microcontrollers, firmware, software applications and machine learning. What excites me most is combining hardware and software to create practical solutions: devices that sense the world, software that makes sense of it, and systems that act on it.

Today my interests span computer networking, hardware design, embedded systems, software development and emerging technologies, and I'm looking for opportunities to apply them on real-world teams.

Academic journey

Computer engineering gave me two lenses: the precision of hardware (timing, signals, power) and the flexibility of software. Labs taught me to debug patiently with a multimeter and a logic analyzer; projects taught me to plan, collaborate and document. My senior year brings it together in a capstone that applies everything I've learned.

Approach

How I learn, build and solve

01 / Learn

Learn by building

I understand a concept best once I've implemented it: a driver, a datapath, a model. Theory becomes intuition through practice.

02 / Innovate

Start from the need

Good engineering starts with a real problem. I define who it's for and what success looks like before choosing tools.

03 / Solve

Break it down, test it

Complex systems become manageable in small, verifiable pieces. I prototype early, measure and iterate on evidence.

04 / Share

Document & collaborate

Clear documentation and honest communication make teams faster and make work outlive the semester.
